# AI Powered Recognition of Doctor's Medical Handwritten Prescription

## Project Overview
MedTextify AI is an advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) system specifically designed to recognize and digitize doctor's handwritten medical prescriptions. The project aims to solve the real-world challenge of interpreting often difficult-to-read medical prescriptions, reducing medication errors, and improving patient safety.

The system uses a combination of image preprocessing techniques, multiple OCR engines, medical terminology databases, and machine learning to achieve high accuracy in extracting information from prescription images.
